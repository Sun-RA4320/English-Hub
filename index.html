The practical English platform built for Africa and the world. Get exactly what to say, and how to say it.

Adapts to Your Style

Personalized Paths. Our entry quiz detects your level and preferred learning style—visual, auditory, or active—and builds your personal path. No more one-size-fits-all textbooks. We focus on real-world scenarios that actually matter to your daily life and career.

Oral First Approach

Stop Studying, Start Speaking. Fluency is built by speaking, not just reading. We use Shadowing, Voice Journaling, and AI Role-play to force you out of your comfort zone in a safe environment. You don't just learn the word; you learn the rhythm, the tone, and the confidence to use it.

Our Mission

🌍
West African Context

Not Duolingo. Not BBC Learning English. We are contextualized for West Africa. Our scenarios come from Lomé, Accra, and Dakar — not London or New York. Every dialogue, every phrase, every situation is built around the reality you live every day.

🏛️
Built by ISLA University

Behind English Hub is ISLA University, Lomé, Togo. A team of educators, linguists, and engineers who understand the African learner. People trust people, not logos — and this project was built by people who share your context, your challenges, and your ambition.
